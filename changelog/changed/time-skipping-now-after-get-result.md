The documentation of `testing.WorkflowEnvironment.getResult` and `testing.WorkflowEnvironment.now` now states that on the time-skipping server `env.now()` after `getResult` is only no earlier than the run's close, because the server may skip on to its next pending timer before the time lock is taken back, even a canceled `awaitTimeout` timeout of the closed run itself; read `close_time` from `Client.describeWorkflow` for the close time.
