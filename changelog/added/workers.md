`worker.Worker` polls one task queue and runs the workflows and activities registered on it; `Worker.run` runs until `Worker.stop`, `Worker.runUntilSignal` until SIGINT or SIGTERM, and `Worker.waitUntilRunning` waits for polling to start. `run` returns an error when the worker can't start or polling fails fatally.
`worker.Options` sets concurrency limits, pollers, the workflow cache size, identity, the graceful shutdown period, logging, interceptors, and a per-run `workflow_memory_limit`; workflow activations of different runs are processed in parallel.
`worker.Options.deployment` makes a worker a version of a Worker Deployment, with `workflow.VersioningBehavior` keeping each run on its version or moving it to the current one; `build_id` and `use_build_id_versioning` cover build-ID versioning.
