`client.Options.http_connect_proxy` connects through an HTTP CONNECT proxy, with optional basic auth; `client.Options.keep_alive` sets the gRPC keep-alive pings, or turns them off; and `client.Options.worker_heartbeat_interval` sets how often workers heartbeat to the server, or turns heartbeats off. See [Proxy, keep-alive, and worker heartbeats](https://github.com/mikeacjones/temporal-sdk-zig/blob/main/README.md#proxy-keep-alive-and-worker-heartbeats).
