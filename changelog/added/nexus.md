`nexus.OperationDef` and `nexus.ServiceDef` define a Nexus service contract, `nexus.ServiceHandler` binds each operation to a synchronous handler or to a workflow-run handler that returns a `nexus.WorkflowStart`, and `Worker.registerNexusService` registers it; handlers get a `nexus.Context` and fail with `ctx.fail`, `ctx.failCanceled` or `ctx.handlerError`. Workers do not serve registered services yet.
`workflow.Context.executeNexusOperation` and `executeNexusOperationByName` call a Nexus operation through an endpoint and return a `workflow.NexusOperationFuture` for its start, result and cancellation, with `workflow.NexusOperationOptions` for timeouts, headers and the cancellation type; `testing.WorkflowEnvironment.createNexusEndpoint` creates an endpoint on the dev server for tests.
