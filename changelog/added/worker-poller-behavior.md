`worker.Options.workflow_task_poller_behavior`, `activity_task_poller_behavior` and `nexus_task_poller_behavior` take a `worker.PollerBehavior`: a simple maximum of open polls, or autoscaling between a minimum and a maximum from an initial count, following the server's feedback. `worker.Options.nonsticky_to_sticky_poll_ratio` sets the share of workflow task pollers that poll the task queue rather than the worker's sticky queue. Invalid settings make `Worker.run` return `error.InvalidOptions`. See [Poller behavior](https://github.com/mikeacjones/temporal-sdk-zig#poller-behavior).
