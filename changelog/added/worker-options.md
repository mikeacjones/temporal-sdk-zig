`worker.Options` gains `sticky_schedule_to_start_timeout`, `max_activities_per_second`, `max_task_queue_activities_per_second`, `default_heartbeat_throttle_interval`, `max_heartbeat_throttle_interval`, `max_eager_activity_reservations_per_workflow_task` (0 turns eager activities off) and `max_concurrent_local_activity_executions`; `run` returns `error.InvalidOptions` for a rate or duration out of range. See [Workers](https://github.com/mikeacjones/temporal-sdk-zig#workers).
`worker.Options.poll_activity_tasks = false` stops a worker polling its task queue for activity tasks, so its activities run only as local activities of its own workflows.
