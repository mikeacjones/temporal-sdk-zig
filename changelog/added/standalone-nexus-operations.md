**Experimental**: `Client.executeNexusOperation` (and `executeNexusOperationByName`) starts a Nexus operation through an endpoint without a workflow, and returns a `client.NexusOperationHandle`. `getNexusOperationHandle` gets a handle to an operation by its ID, whose `get`, `describe`, `cancel` and `terminate` wait for its result, report its status and end it, and `listNexusOperations` and `countNexusOperations` find operations with a visibility query. See [Standalone operations](https://github.com/mikeacjones/temporal-sdk-zig/blob/main/README.md#standalone-operations).
