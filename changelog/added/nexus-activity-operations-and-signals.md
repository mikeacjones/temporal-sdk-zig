**Experimental**: Nexus operation handlers can back an asynchronous operation with a standalone activity by returning `nexus.ActivityStart`, and can signal or signal-with-start a workflow once per request with `nexus.Context.signalWorkflow` and `signalWithStartWorkflow`, deduplicated by the request's ID; a second signal in one request returns `error.SignalAlreadySent`. The started activity, the signaled workflow and the operation's response carry links, and `testing.DevServer.activity_callback_args` and `testing.DevServer.signal_link_args` enable what the dev server needs; see [Activity-backed operations and signals](https://github.com/mikeacjones/temporal-sdk-zig/blob/main/README.md#activity-backed-operations-and-signals).
