**Experimental**: payload codecs, the payload converter's context hooks and the failure converter get a Nexus operation's endpoint, service and operation in `converter.Context.nexus` (`converter.NexusContext`), on the caller's worker and the handler's, so a codec can pick a key per Nexus endpoint; `Client.fetchHistory` and `historyIterator` decode those events with the same contexts, and `workflow.NexusOperationFuture.converterContext` returns the operation's. A workflow, update or activity started for a Nexus operation, when the server propagates the caller's context with its start, converts its result and failure (and an activity its heartbeat details) with it; `activity.Info.nexus_context`, `activity.Context.resultConverterContext`, `nexus.Context.converterContext`, `client.ActivityCompletionOptions` with `Client.completeActivityWithOptions` and the other `...WithOptions` completion calls, `client.ActivityId.nexus_context`, and `testing.DevServer.nexus_context_cli_version` with `testing.WorkflowEnvironment.Options.dev_server_cli_version` go with it; see [Payload codecs](https://github.com/mikeacjones/temporal-sdk-zig/blob/main/README.md#payload-codecs).
