A type may declare `toPayloadWithContext` and `fromPayloadWithContext` to receive a `converter.Context` (namespace, workflow ID and type, activity type and ID, propagated values) for the call its value belongs to; the SDK passes it for client, workflow, activity, child workflow and standalone activity conversions, and `converter.toPayloadWithContext`, `converter.fromPayloadWithContext`, `EncodedValues.withContext` and the `converterContext()` accessors on workflow and activity contexts and futures pass it for failure details. See [Payloads](https://github.com/mikeacjones/temporal-sdk-zig/blob/main/README.md#payloads).
