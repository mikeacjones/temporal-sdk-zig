`testing.LogRecorder` is a thread-safe `log.Sink` for tests: give its `sink()` to `WorkflowEnvironment.startWithOptions(.{ .logger = ... })` or `ActivityEnvironment.logger`, and a workflow or activity can log at error level without failing the Zig test, which `std.log` does; `records(arena)` returns copies of every record, `count(level, message)` counts them, and `Record.get(key)` reads a field. The SDK's own error logs, such as worker poll or shutdown failures, still go to `std.log`.
