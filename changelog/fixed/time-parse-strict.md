`converter.Time.parse`, and with it decoding a `Time` from JSON, now returns `error.InvalidTime` for a day the month doesn't have (2023-02-29, 2024-04-31), an offset hour above 23 or minute above 59 (`+00:99`), a leap second (`:60`), a field with anything but ASCII digits (`20_3`), and a time the offset moves outside the years 0000 to 9999 in UTC, where it used to return a different instant.
