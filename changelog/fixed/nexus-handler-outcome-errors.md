A Nexus handler that returns the outcome error of a client wait, such as `error.UpdateFailed` from `WorkflowUpdateHandle.get` or `error.WorkflowFailed` from `WorkflowRun.get`, now fails the request with a non-retryable `internal` handler error instead of a retried one, so the caller's operation fails at once rather than being retried until its timeout; when the wait was given `nexus.Context.allocator()`, the outcome's failure is in the caller's cause chain (see [Services and handlers](https://github.com/mikeacjones/temporal-sdk-zig#services-and-handlers)).
