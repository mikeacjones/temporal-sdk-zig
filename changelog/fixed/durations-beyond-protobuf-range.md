A duration or time too large for protobuf no longer panics the worker or client: `Context.sleep(.max, ...)`, `Context.awaitTimeout`, sessions, activity, local activity, child workflow, start, continue-as-new and schedule timeouts, retry policy intervals, `Application.next_retry_delay` and `WorkflowEnvironment.sleep` send the longest duration protobuf can carry (about 10,000 years) instead, schedule times outside 0001-01-01 to 9999-12-31 UTC are sent as that bound, and the OpenTelemetry `periodicity` is clamped to `maxInt(u32)` ms.
A negative duration's whole and fractional seconds now carry the same sign, as protobuf requires: -1.5 s is sent as -1 s and -0.5 s, not -2 s and +0.5 s.
