`Client.callService` now returns `error.InvalidArgument` for a request with a string field that isn't valid UTF-8, without making a call, and its `RpcFailure.message` or warning names the field by its proto path, such as `execution.workflow_id`; it returned `error.RpcFailed` with "gRPC status 0" and no field. The client's typed calls, such as `startWorkflow` and `signalWorkflow`, check their requests the same way and fail as for a server's INVALID_ARGUMENT, naming the field.
