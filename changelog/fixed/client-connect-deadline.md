`Client.connect` now gives up on a server that doesn't answer, such as one that accepts the connection but never completes the TLS handshake, after `Options.rpc_timeout` (30 s without one), returning `error.ConnectFailed` with the reason logged; it used to wait forever. Its runtime is kept with the attempt sdk-core still makes, and a later `connect` with the same options waits on that attempt instead of starting another; at most 4 are kept per process, and while 4 are and none matches, `connect` fails at once.
A client from `Client.init` keeps at most 4 connection attempts in flight against such a server, where it used to start another with every call.
