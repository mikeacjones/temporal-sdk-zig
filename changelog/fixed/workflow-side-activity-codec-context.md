Payload codecs on the workflow's worker now get an activity's or local activity's `converter.Context`, with its type and ID, for its arguments, result and failure, and a child workflow's ID and type for the child's, as the worker that runs it does; before, they got the run's context, so a codec keyed on `activity_type` couldn't decode what the other side encoded. Signals to other workflows keep the run's context, and a side effect's value now gets the run's context on both workers.
