`Worker.run` returns `error.InvalidOptions`, and logs why, when `worker.Options.workflow_stack_size` is below the new `worker.min_workflow_stack_size` (64 KiB) or too large to round up to whole pages plus a guard page; before, such a size was accepted and the worker crashed with a segmentation fault at its first workflow task (or ran on a stack that small). Set `workflow_stack_size` to at least 64 KiB, or leave the 256 KiB default.
