Any number of coroutines may now wait on the same `workflow.ActivityFuture`, `ChildWorkflowFuture`, `NexusOperationFuture`, `SignalExternalFuture` or `CancelExternalFuture`, and all of them return once it resolves, in the order they began waiting; before, only the coroutine that began waiting last was woken, and the others stayed blocked for good. A history recorded while a coroutine was stranded that way now replays with that coroutine going on, so it may fail with a nondeterminism error: finish such runs on workers of the previous release, or put what that coroutine does after the wait behind `workflow.Context.patched` so their replay keeps the old path.
